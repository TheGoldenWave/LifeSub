## Design Context

### Users

LifeSub serves privacy-conscious macOS users who record meetings, conversations, and long-form spoken material for later verification. They need to start and stop capture with confidence, understand whether evidence is safe, find an exact spoken passage quickly, and return from text to its source audio without learning a technical evidence system.

### Brand Personality

Quiet, trustworthy, and humane. The product should feel like a careful archivist and a discreet narrator: calm during long-running capture, precise when presenting evidence, and explicit when permissions or processing fail.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a dark-first, monochrome evidence-terminal interface: a blue-gray near-black canvas, off-white text, sharp borders, flat surfaces, and generous structural whitespace. Monospace typography carries controls, timestamps, status, and Evidence identifiers; a refined Chinese sans-serif carries transcripts and explanations. Functional color appears only for recording, pause, success, error, and focus. Avoid shadows, gradients, large radii, glass effects, decorative analytics, and consumer-AI neon styling.

### Design Principles

1. Make recording state unmistakable at every moment.
2. Keep source, timestamp, revision, and evidence status visually attached.
3. Present advanced provenance progressively, without hiding it.
4. Use flat dark surfaces and high-contrast typography to support long reading sessions.
5. Treat empty, loading, permission, and failure states as first-class product moments.
