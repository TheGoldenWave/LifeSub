### Task 6: Helper Authentication And Supervision

Complete and approved, with no remaining findings.

- Private launch directory is `0700`; socket is `0600`.
- Nonce is generated by the parent and delivered only through inherited FD 3.
- Nonce is absent from argv/env/runtime paths and is zeroized by guards.
- Peer UID, kernel-reported PID, spawned PID and `proc_pidpath` must match.

- One absolute handshake deadline covers accept, identity and hello.
